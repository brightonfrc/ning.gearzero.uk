Our mission statement

At Gear Ning, our mission is to inspire innovation, teamwork, and a lifelong passion for STEM through competitive robotics. We strive to design, build, and program with creativity and purpose, while learning from every challenge and supporting one another along the way.

We believe robotics is more than building a machine—it’s about building people. Through collaboration, perseverance, and gracious professionalism, we aim to develop confident problem-solvers, responsible leaders, and enthusiastic engineers who make a positive impact in our team, our community, and beyond.

Build boldly. Learn constantly. Compete graciously. Grow together. Ning Zhao.
